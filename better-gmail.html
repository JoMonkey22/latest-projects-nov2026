Better Gmail

An AI side panel for Gmail and Google Chat that helps you understand what needs attention and draft the next reply.

What it does

Summarizes the email you open, organizes the day’s inbox into priorities, drafts replies in selectable tones, and surfaces available Google Chat previews. Drafted replies go into Gmail’s own editor; the user presses Send.

Recent improvements

Unread Chat status follows Google’s loaded conversation list without opening background conversations or changing read status. Chat groups organize conversations into Friends, School, Clubs, Apps and custom assignments. Summary caches stay separate across Google accounts.

Mail-management actions such as archiving, starring and labeling are presented for confirmation. The extension does not automatically send messages.
